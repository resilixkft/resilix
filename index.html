Services

Resilix designs, plans, and delivers datacenter solutions built around one goal: systems that stay reliable under real-world pressure. We work across the full lifecycle of your infrastructure, from the first assessment through to hands-on delivery and beyond.

Our services

L1 and L2 maintenance support

Our L1 and L2 teams handle day-to-day incidents and escalations, keeping your systems running and responding quickly whenever something needs attention.

Assessment of existing infrastructure

We evaluate what's already in place — hardware, network topology, security posture, backup coverage, and overall system health — for a clear, honest picture of where things stand and where the risks are hiding.

IT infrastructure planning and delivery

We translate findings into a concrete roadmap and take it from paper to production, configuring and deploying systems with the same accountability throughout, so what works today still works in three years.

Hardening

We harden systems at the network, server, and access layers, closing common attack paths and reducing exposure before issues can turn into incidents.

Whether you're modernizing legacy infrastructure, building a new environment from scratch, or shoring up gaps in an existing setup, our approach stays the same: assess honestly, plan deliberately, and deliver systems you can actually depend on.

Focus areas

Networking

We design and implement network architectures that are fast, segmented, and resilient to failure, whether that means a redesigned core switching layer or a rebuilt WAN strategy.

Security

Security runs through everything we touch rather than sitting as an afterthought bolted on at the end. We harden systems at the network, server, and access layers, and build monitoring into deployments so issues get caught early.

Backup & Disaster Recovery

Backup and disaster recovery are treated as first-class requirements, not optional add-ons — we design backup strategies that are tested, verified, and actually restorable when you need them.

Servers & Storage

Our server work spans physical and virtualized environments, from initial provisioning to ongoing performance tuning and lifecycle management. On the storage side, we help clients choose the right architecture for their workload, balancing performance, capacity, and cost.

Throughout every project, our focus stays on resilience: systems designed to absorb failure gracefully instead of collapsing under it. We don't disappear after go-live — we stay accountable for what we build, because a datacenter solution is only as good as its performance six months or six years down the line.
